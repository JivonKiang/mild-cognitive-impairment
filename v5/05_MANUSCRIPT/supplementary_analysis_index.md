Each supplementary panel must carry the label `computational hypothesis
generation`, `orthogonal context`, `cell-type-specific context`, or
`exploratory paired molecular-response audit` in its legend. The primary
conclusion must remain anchored to leakage-aware evaluation and independent
transportability.
